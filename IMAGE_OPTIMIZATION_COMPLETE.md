## What You Need to Do

### When Adding Images to Your Site

**Step 1**: Put image in `src/assets/` folder
```
src/
  assets/
    your-image.jpg  ← Put your image here
```

**Step 2**: Use in your .astro files
```astro
---
import OptimizedImage from '../components/OptimizedImage.astro';
import myImage from '../assets/your-image.jpg';
---

<OptimizedImage
  src={myImage}
  alt="Description of image"
  width={800}
  height={600}
/>
```

**Step 3**: That's it! ✅
- Automatically converted to WebP
- Automatically compressed (no quality loss)
- Automatically lazy loaded
- Automatically responsive
