# Section Numbering Structure

All sections on the Boundless landing page are now properly numbered and titled for clear navigation and hierarchy.

## Section Order

1. **Hero Section** (No number)
   - First impression/main value proposition

2. **01 / The Problem**
   - Component: `ProblemSection.tsx`
   - Highlights pain points painting contractors face

3. **02 / The Solution**
   - Component: `SolutionSection.tsx`
   - Presents the all-in-one growth solution

4. **03 / How It Works**
   - Component: `HowItWorks.tsx`
   - 4-step process from zero to booked

5. **04 / The Results**
   - Component: `ResultsSection.tsx`
   - Quantifiable outcomes and metrics

6. **05 / What Our Clients Say**
   - Component: `TestimonialsSection.tsx`
   - Social proof and testimonials

7. **06 / Limited Time Offer**
   - Component: `OfferSection.tsx`
   - Special pricing and what's included

8. **07 / Pricing Plans**
   - Component: `PricingSection.tsx`
   - Three-tier pricing structure

9. **08 / Frequently Asked Questions**
   - Component: `FAQSection.tsx`
   - Common questions and answers

10. **09 / Ready to Transform Your Business?**
    - Component: `CTASection.tsx`
    - Final booking form

## Styling Consistency

Each section title follows the format:
```
<div className="font-mono text-xs tracking-wider uppercase mb-4" style={{ color: '#a28b6d' }}>
  [NUMBER] / [TITLE]
</div>
```

This creates a consistent, professional hierarchy throughout the entire page.
